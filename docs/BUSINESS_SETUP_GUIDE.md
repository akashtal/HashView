# Business Setup Guide
Welcome to the HashView QR Ordering Platform!

## 1. Configure Your Menu
1. Log into the HashView dashboard.
2. Navigate to **Menu Management**.
3. Create Categories and Items. Ensure `isAvailable` is checked.
4. Set up Modifiers (e.g., "Size: Small/Large") and Add-ons.

## 2. Set Up Billing Rules
1. Navigate to **Billing & Taxes**.
2. Add your local Tax rates (e.g., "VAT 20%").
3. Add any standard Service Charges if applicable.
4. Specify if your menu prices are *Tax Inclusive* or *Tax Exclusive*.

## 3. Print QR Codes
1. Navigate to **Table Management**.
2. Generate QR codes for your tables. 
3. Print and display them. Customers who scan these will automatically have their `tableNumber` attached to their order!
